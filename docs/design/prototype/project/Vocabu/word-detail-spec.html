Word detail — read layout

Everything the entry is, in one centred column you can read without touching anything. The word is the hero; the person, the meaning, the story and the date orbit it in decreasing weight. Social is not part of v0 — what's left is a page you open to remember, not to respond.

The decisions

Q1 · what shape is the page
One centred column

No cards, no left-aligned rows, no chrome around the entry. The detail is a page of paper with the word in the middle — the feed's card opened up and given air.

Q2 · is the story open or folded
Open by default

You came here for the story; making it a second tap is a toll. The toggle stays so a long story can be collapsed back down to the word — the fold is a reader's tool, not a gate.

Q3 · what happens to the social row
Cut from v0, whole

Like, save, reply and the reply composer all leave together. A half-social detail (a heart with nothing behind it) is worse than none. The layout below is complete without them.

Everything is centred, including the story paragraph. It's the one place in the app where centred body copy is right: the block is short, the page is a keepsake, and the column is built around the word's axis.

Behaviour

CaseBehaviour
Open / closeCross-fade over the feed, --dur-base. Back returns to the same scroll position in the feed.
Unfold toggleOpen on mount. State is per-visit, not persisted — reopening the word shows the story again.
Long wordShrinks through the five steps, then wraps. Never truncated, never ellipsised — the whole phrase is the entry.
Long storyPage scrolls. No max-height, no “read more”.
Media failurePlayer renders its error state in place; the rest of the layout is unaffected. See media spec.
Someone else's entryIdentical layout, no ⋯, no edit on the date.
Reduced motionChevron rotation and the open cross-fade drop to instant.

Open for product

  • Where social returns. When it does, it re-enters below the said-on row behind the same hairline the prototype used — the read stack above it doesn't move.
  • On-this-day framing. Arriving from a resurfaced memory may want a small overline above the avatar; not specced, not blocking.
  • Sibling navigation. Swiping between words in a collection is the obvious next gesture and the reason the detail has no fixed bottom chrome.
