Entry edit & delete

Keeping is the loop; changing what you kept is the exception. Both actions live behind one quiet ⋯ on the word detail — the only screen where you're looking at a single entry with intent. Edit reuses the compose sheet you already know. Delete asks once, plainly, and means it.

rare by designcompose reuseone confirm, no undo theater30 Jul 2026

The decisions

Q1 · where do the actions live
⋯ menu on word detail

Rare actions earn a quiet home. The feed stays a reading surface — no per-card affordances, no accidental taps. Detail is where you've already committed attention to one entry.

Q2 · what is the edit surface
The compose sheet, prefilled

Same fields, same order, same muscle memory. Title flips to Edit word, the button to Save changes. Nothing new to learn; nothing to keep in sync with compose as it evolves.

Q3 · how careful is delete
One honest confirm

A small sheet names the word and what leaves with it — the story, the voice. One tap to confirm, one to back out. No typing the word to confirm, no fake-undo toast for v0.

Entry points, weighed

ProposalVerdictWhy
⋯ menu on detailShipped — defaultZero visual cost at rest; both actions in one place; room for future items (share, move to collection) without redesign.
Pencil + trash in the barShipped — behind a tweakOne tap faster, but a permanently visible trash on a keepsake reads harsh, and two icons crowd the 54px bar. Kept as the icons variant for side-by-side judgment.
Swipe left/right on touchRejectedUndiscoverable with no affordance; horizontal swipe collides with the browser/PWA back gesture on iOS; and destructive-on-swipe is the opposite of “quiet interactive elements”. If a swipe ever ships it belongs on feed rows, revealing buttons — never firing actions directly.

Edit: what moves, what holds

Editing a word never rewrites its history. The social record and the moment anchor survive every edit — only the content the user is looking at changes.

FieldOn save
word · meaning · storyReplaced with the sheet's values. Revealed fields open pre-filled; empty optional fields stay hidden behind their add-chips.
speakerRe-pointable. The chip row opens with the current person selected; picking another re-derives relation and frozen age from the same saidAt.
voice / videoShown in its kept state, removable, replaceable. Removing it and saving deletes the media.
collectionRe-fileable like any compose.
saidAtUntouched. Edited only in place on the detail screen (see speaker spec) — compose never shows a date.
id · likes · replies · savedUntouched. An edit is not a repost; the entry keeps its place in the feed.

Flow

  1. Detail → ⋯ → edit word. The compose sheet rises over the detail, every present field filled.
  2. Change anything → Save changes. The sheet drops; the detail underneath is already updated — no navigation, no scroll loss.
  3. Cancel abandons cleanly; the entry is untouched.

The ellipsis on delete… is the standard promise that a confirm follows — nothing destructive happens on the first tap. Terracotta (--danger) appears exactly twice: the menu item and the confirm button. No “are you sure?”, no “successfully deleted”.

Not in v0 · open for product

  • Undo window. A 5-second “deleted — undo” toast instead of a confirm is the friendlier long-term shape; it needs soft-delete server-side, so the confirm sheet holds the line for now.
  • Edit history. Deliberately none. A keepsake shows its current truth, not a changelog.
  • Share / move actions. The ⋯ menu is where they'd land — the container is built for growth.
  • Feed swipe. If ever, swipe reveals buttons on feed rows (edit/delete) rather than acting directly — and only after the PWA owns its back gesture.
